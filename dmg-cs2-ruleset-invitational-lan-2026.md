## Eligibility

### Minimum Age

All participants must be at least 15 years of age at the time of the first day of tournament play.  
_Note: some venues may have a separate Minor Policy due to liquor licensing requirements._

### Region

Regional requirements differ by pathway. See the [Additional Information document](dfrag-invitational-lan-additional-info-formatted.md) for full detail.

#### Direct VRS Invites

- Oceania Direct VRS Invites use the Oceania VRS list.
- Asia Direct VRS Invites use the Asia VRS list.
- Direct VRS Invited Rosters to LAN must be able to attend **The DFRAG Studio** from **October 26 – November 1, 2026**.

#### Online Open Qualifier

- Online Open Qualifier matches are hosted on **FACEIT** (servers in Sydney, Australia). FACEIT Anti-Cheat is required.
- Sydney is the online server location only and does not define Open Qualifier eligibility.
- Participants must reside in or be able to compete from **Asia**.
- The Online Open Qualifier awards Online Closed Qualifier slots only and does not award Offline LAN Main Event slots directly.

#### Online Closed Qualifier

- Online Closed Qualifier matches are hosted on **FACEIT** (servers in Sydney, Australia). FACEIT Anti-Cheat is required.
- The Online Closed Qualifier contains no Direct VRS Invites and is filled by the top 8 eligible Online Open Qualifier teams.
- Rosters that qualify through the Online Closed Qualifier must be able to attend the Offline LAN Main Event if they advance.

### Existing Ban

Any participant with an active ban from FACEIT, ESL, Valve, or another recognised integrity body is prohibited from participating in the tournament.

**Disqualification Rules:** All disqualification rules are published in this ruleset and the [Additional Information document](dfrag-invitational-lan-additional-info-formatted.md) as required by Valve Tournament Operation Requirements. Any disqualification decisions will be published at the time of enforcement. Rules are objective, transparent, and applied equally to all participants.

## Team

### Restrictions

#### In-Game Names (Alias)

Participants may not use any in-game name (Alias) which:

- Contains vulgar or offensive language
- Contains a sponsor or product name/short name
- Is already in use by another participant

The Tournament Organiser may restrict an Alias at any time, including for reasons outside those listed above.

#### Multiple Teams

Participants are restricted from being on more than one team at a time.

#### Team Size

A team will consist of:

- At least 5 Players
- Up to 2 Substitute Players
- 1 Coach

All members must be eligible under **Rule 2 – Eligibility**.

### Team Continuity

- DFRAG INVITATIONAL 2026 issues Direct VRS Invites.
- For Direct VRS Invited Rosters, Core Members are the five (5) Athletes on the Invited Roster as of the Invitation Date.
- A forfeit will be declared in any match where a Direct VRS Invited Roster does not field at least three of the Invited Roster Athletes for the entirety of the match.
- For teams entering through the Online Open Qualifier or Online Closed Qualifier, Core Members are the five (5) players who originally qualified or were seeded into that pathway.
- Teams must field at least three (3) Core Members for the entirety of each official match.
- Failure to field at least three (3) Core Members may result in a match forfeit.
- Substitutions must not be used to misrepresent roster identity, bypass eligibility requirements, or manipulate VRS invite acceptance.

### Substitutes

- Must be added at least 24 hours before match start.
- May only be used before the first round of record of a map.
- Coaches cannot be used as substitutes.

### Emergency Substitutes

In extraordinary circumstances (medical issue, power outage, etc.), an unlisted substitute may be allowed at the organiser’s discretion.

### Coaches

- Coaches are not permitted in the server during open qualifiers
- May only be used as substitutes in emergencies.

### Extrajudicial Agreements

“Gentlemen’s Agreements” are not sanctioned or enforceable by the organiser.

### Team Transfer

Participants may transfer teams after the tournament starts only with organiser approval.

### Main Event Roster Lock

- Registration / Roster Lock is **October 25, 2026, 23:59 AEST**.
- All Offline LAN Main Event rosters must be submitted and confirmed before Registration / Roster Lock.
- After Registration / Roster Lock, roster changes are not permitted except for approved emergency substitutes under the Emergency Substitutes rule.
- Roster Lock applies to all teams with a confirmed Offline LAN Main Event slot, including Direct VRS Invited Rosters and Closed Qualifier teams.

## Registration and Qualification

DFRAG INVITATIONAL 2026 is a Ranked Tier 2 tournament with **6 Direct VRS Invites** and **no Wildcard Invites**.

### Direct VRS Invites

- **Invitation Date:** August 21, 2026.
- Direct VRS Invites use the **August 2026** official VRS publication current as of the Invitation Date.
- **Total Direct VRS Invites:** 6
  - **4 Oceania** Direct VRS Invites to Offline LAN Main Event
  - **2 Asia** Direct VRS Invites to Offline LAN Main Event
- All Direct VRS Invites enter the Offline LAN Main Event at the same stage.
- Invitations are issued sequentially from each announced VRS list, starting with the highest-ranked eligible roster on that list.
- Oceania LAN Direct VRS Invites are filled from the Oceania list.
- Asia LAN Direct VRS Invites are filled from the Asia list.
- Invited Rosters must be ranked lower than 12 on the Global VRS at the Invitation Date.
- Invited Rosters have **48 hours** from invite issue to **acknowledge** the invite, and a further **48 hours** after acknowledgement to **accept** the invite (**4 days total** from invite issue).
- If an Invited Roster does not acknowledge within 48 hours, does not accept within the further 48 hours after acknowledgement, cannot accept, or declines, invitations proceed down the relevant VRS list in order until all announced slots are filled or the supplementation rules below apply.
- Direct VRS Invited Rosters may only accept if:
  - at least three Athletes from the Invited Roster will play each match
  - the Participating Roster satisfies the invite criteria
  - the roster has not previously declined an invitation to the same tournament
- If either or both of the **2 Asia** Direct VRS Invite LAN slots cannot be filled after proceeding sequentially down the Asia VRS list, each unfilled Asia LAN slot will be converted into an additional Offline LAN Main Event slot awarded through the Online Closed Qualifier.
- All Direct VRS Invited Rosters to LAN must be able to attend **The DFRAG Studio** from **October 26 – November 1, 2026**.
- Acceptance of a Direct VRS Invite to the Offline LAN Main Event is a binding commitment to attend the LAN in person. See [Prize Forfeiture and Financial Fines](#prize-forfeiture-and-financial-fines).

### Online Open Qualifier

- Online Open Qualifier registration opens on **August 7, 2026**.
- Online Open Qualifier registration closes on **August 30, 2026**.
- The Online Open Qualifier will begin on **August 31, 2026**.
- The Online Open Qualifier awards **8 Online Closed Qualifier slots**.
- The Online Open Qualifier does **not** award Offline LAN Main Event slots directly.
- Online Open Qualifier matches are hosted on **FACEIT** (servers in Sydney, Australia).
- Sydney is the online server location only and does not define Open Qualifier eligibility.
- Entry into the Online Open Qualifier is free.
- FACEIT Anti-Cheat is required for all Online Open Qualifier matches.
- The Online Open Qualifier is open only to registered rosters based in, or able to compete from, **Asia**.
- Merely connecting to Sydney servers from outside Asia does not satisfy Online Open Qualifier eligibility requirements.
- Online Open Qualifier registration order and timestamps will be publicly inspectable via FACEIT.
- The Online Open Qualifier will be double-elimination.
- Matches will be Best-of-1 until qualification matches.
- Qualification matches will be Best-of-3.
- The top **8** eligible rosters from the Online Open Qualifier will advance to the Online Closed Qualifier.

### Online Closed Qualifier

- The Online Closed Qualifier will be held on **September 7–8, 2026**.
- Closed Qualifier roster lock occurs after Online Open Qualifier results are confirmed, and before **September 7, 2026**.
- The Online Closed Qualifier contains no Direct VRS Invites.
- The Online Closed Qualifier will feature **8** teams: the top 8 eligible Online Open Qualifier teams.
- The Online Closed Qualifier will be seeded using the **August 2026** VRS publication date specified in the Additional Information document.
- Rosters without a Global VRS rank cannot be seeded above VRS-ranked rosters.
- Online Closed Qualifier matches are hosted on **FACEIT** (servers in Sydney, Australia).
- FACEIT Anti-Cheat is required for all Online Closed Qualifier matches.
- The Online Closed Qualifier will be double-elimination.
- Match format is as set out in this ruleset and the Additional Information document.
- The Online Closed Qualifier awards **2** Offline LAN Main Event slots by default.
- If one or both Asia Direct VRS Invite LAN slots cannot be filled, additional Offline LAN Main Event slots will be awarded from the Online Closed Qualifier.
- The top finishing eligible rosters from the Online Closed Qualifier will advance to the Offline LAN Main Event according to the number of LAN slots awarded through this pathway.

### Offline LAN Main Event

- The Offline LAN Main Event will be held at **The DFRAG Studio** from **October 26 – November 1, 2026**.
- The Offline LAN Main Event will feature 8 teams:
  - 4 Oceania Direct VRS Invites
  - 2 Asia Direct VRS Invites, or additional Closed Qualifier LAN slots if Asia Direct VRS Invite slots cannot be filled
  - 2 teams qualified through the Online Closed Qualifier by default, plus any additional Closed Qualifier LAN slots awarded under the Asia supplementation rule
- LAN format:
  - Group stage: October 26–28, 2026 — double-elimination, 2 groups of 4
  - Playoffs: October 30 – November 1, 2026 — 4-team double-elimination with a third-place match
  - Grand Final: November 1, 2026 — Best-of-5
- Any roster that accepts a Direct VRS Invite to the Offline LAN Main Event, or that receives a confirmed Offline LAN Main Event slot through the Online Closed Qualifier, must attend the LAN in person at **The DFRAG Studio** during the published LAN dates.
- Failure to travel to or attend the Offline LAN Main Event after accepting an invite or confirming a LAN slot is subject to [Prize Forfeiture and Financial Fines](#prize-forfeiture-and-financial-fines), regardless of when notice is given.

### Vacancies and Replacements

Direct VRS Invited Rosters have **48 hours** from invite issue to acknowledge the invite, and a further **48 hours** after acknowledgement to accept the invite (**4 days total** from invite issue).

If a Direct VRS Invited Roster does not acknowledge within 48 hours, does not accept within the further 48 hours after acknowledgement, declines, cannot accept, fails eligibility checks, or fails roster validation, the vacant invite slot will be offered to the next highest-ranked eligible roster on the same announced VRS list, proceeding sequentially down the list.

If either or both of the **2 Asia** Direct VRS Invite LAN slots cannot be filled after proceeding sequentially down the Asia VRS list under the acknowledgement and acceptance process, each unfilled Asia LAN slot will be converted into an additional Offline LAN Main Event slot awarded through the Online Closed Qualifier.

If a roster qualified through the Online Open Qualifier withdraws prior to the start of the Online Closed Qualifier, the vacant Closed Qualifier slot will be filled by the next highest-placing eligible roster from the Online Open Qualifier, as determined by final standings and predefined tiebreakers.

If a roster qualified through the Online Closed Qualifier withdraws prior to the start of the Offline LAN Main Event, the vacant LAN slot will be filled by the next highest-placing eligible roster from the Online Closed Qualifier, as determined by final standings and predefined tiebreakers.

If no eligible roster is available from the relevant qualifier standings, the Tournament Organiser will select a replacement based on the highest-performing non-qualified roster from that qualifier using objective performance metrics, in this order:

1. final qualifier placement
2. match record
3. map differential
4. round differential
5. head-to-head result, where applicable

No discretionary invites will be issued.

## Fair Play & Active Participation

- Teams must field **five (5) active, non-AFK players** in the server at all times during official matches.
- Intentionally playing with fewer than five active players, or deliberately idling/AFK’ing to manipulate match conditions, is strictly prohibited.
- Abuse of this rule (e.g., intentionally stalling, refusing to play, or otherwise undermining the competitive integrity of a match) will be considered a violation of the **spirit of fair competition**.
- At the organiser’s discretion, violations may result in **round forfeits, map forfeits, disqualification, or additional penalties** as outlined in the [Penalties](#penalties) section.

---

## Match Forfeiture

- Teams are expected to complete all scheduled matches in good faith.
- **Deliberate forfeits** (intentionally abandoning or surrendering entire matches without valid reason) are strictly prohibited.
- Acceptable reasons for forfeiting (e.g., verified technical failure, medical emergency) must be communicated to the organiser immediately and approved at their discretion.
- Abuse of forfeiture (e.g., skipping matches to influence seeding, avoid opponents, or manipulate standings) will be treated as a violation of the **spirit of fair competition**.
- Penalties for deliberate forfeits may include:
  - **Loss of seeding or ranking points**
  - **Disqualification from the current tournament**
  - **Suspension from future events**
  - **Prize forfeiture** as outlined in the [Penalties](#penalties) section

## Sportsmanship

### Respectful Conduct

All participants must treat teammates, opponents, officials, and staff with respect at all times. Abusive language, harassment, threats, or discriminatory remarks of any kind will not be tolerated.

### Banter

Limited competitive banter is permitted only under the following conditions:

- Banter must remain **in-server** during gameplay and must not extend past the match or outside the game server.
- Banter must not attack, demean, or target a person’s:
  - self or personal identity
  - gender
  - race
  - views or beliefs
  - body or physical appearance
- Banter that is discriminatory, harassing, threatening, sexualised, or otherwise abusive is prohibited at all times.
- Banter published on Discord, social media, streams, chats outside the match server, or any other platform after gameplay is prohibited.
- First breaches may result in a warning.
- Repeat offenders will receive **financial fines**, which may be deducted from current, outstanding, or future prize pool payouts for this tournament or future DFRAG events, and may also receive further penalties under [Penalties](#penalties).

### Competitive Integrity

Participants must always play to the best of their ability. Intentionally losing, trolling, or griefing undermines the competition and may result in penalties.

### Professionalism

Participants are representatives of the community. Any conduct bringing the tournament, organiser, or sponsors into disrepute (in-game or publicly on social media) may result in disciplinary action.

### Enforcement

Breaches of sportsmanship are subject to immediate review by tournament officials. Penalties range from warnings and round losses to match forfeits, financial fines, prize forfeiture, suspensions, or permanent bans, as deemed appropriate under [Penalties](#penalties).
