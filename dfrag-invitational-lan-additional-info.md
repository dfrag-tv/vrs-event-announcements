## Tournament Format

### Summary

- **Total teams at LAN:** 8

- **Online stages:** Stage 1 Online Open Qualifier and Stage 2 Online Closed Qualifier
- **LAN stages:** Stage 3 Offline LAN Group Stage and Stage 4 Offline LAN Playoffs

- **Stage type:** Online
- **Teams:** Eligible Asia registered rosters

- **Match format:** As set out in the official ruleset

## Qualification Pathways

| Pathway | Slots | Scope | Leads To | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct VRS Invite (Oceania) | 4 | Oceania | Offline LAN Main Event | Direct VRS Invite |

### Pathway A - Direct VRS Invites to Offline LAN Main Event

#### Slots

- **4 Oceania** Direct VRS Invites enter the Offline LAN Main Event directly.
- **2 Asia** Direct VRS Invites enter the Offline LAN Main Event directly.
- All Direct VRS Invites enter the Offline LAN Main Event at the same stage.

#### Invitation timing and VRS source

- Invitations are issued on the **Invitation Date** of **August 21, 2026**.
- Invitations use the **August 2026** official VRS publication current as of the Invitation Date.
- Oceania Direct VRS Invites to LAN are issued sequentially from the Oceania VRS list, starting with the highest-ranked eligible roster.
- Asia Direct VRS Invites to LAN are issued sequentially from the Asia VRS list, starting with the highest-ranked eligible roster.
- Invited Rosters must be ranked lower than 12 on the Global VRS at the Invitation Date.

#### Acknowledgement and acceptance

- Invited Rosters have **48 hours** from invite issue to **acknowledge** the invite, and a further **48 hours** after acknowledgement to **accept** the invite (**4 days total** from invite issue).
- If an Invited Roster does not acknowledge within 48 hours, does not accept within the further 48 hours after acknowledgement, cannot accept, or declines, the vacant slot will be offered to the next highest-ranked eligible roster on the same announced VRS list, proceeding sequentially down the list.
- Direct VRS Invited Rosters may only accept if at least three Athletes from the Invited Roster will play each match, the Participating Roster satisfies the invite criteria, and the roster has not previously declined an invitation to the same tournament.
- All Direct VRS Invited Rosters to LAN must be able to attend the Offline LAN Main Event in person at **DFRAG STUDIOS** from **October 26 – November 1, 2026**.

#### Unfilled Asia LAN slots

- If either or both of the **2 Asia** Direct VRS Invite LAN slots cannot be filled from the Asia VRS list under the acknowledgement and acceptance process above, each unfilled Asia LAN slot will be converted into an additional Offline LAN Main Event slot awarded through the Online Closed Qualifier.
- The Online Closed Qualifier will then award **more than 2** Offline LAN Main Event slots equal to the number of unfilled Asia slots (for example, 1 unfilled Asia slot means 3 Closed Qualifier LAN slots; 2 unfilled Asia slots means 4 Closed Qualifier LAN slots).

### Pathway B - Online Open Qualifier

- The Online Open Qualifier awards **8** Online Closed Qualifier slots.
- The Online Open Qualifier does **not** award Offline LAN Main Event slots directly.
- The Online Open Qualifier is restricted to eligible Asia rosters.
- The Online Open Qualifier is not a global qualifier.
- Full Online Open Qualifier rules are set out in **Open Qualifier** below.

### Pathway C - Online Closed Qualifier to LAN

- The Online Closed Qualifier contains no Direct VRS Invites.
- The Online Closed Qualifier field is **8 teams**: the top 8 eligible rosters from the Online Open Qualifier.
- The Online Closed Qualifier is seeded using the **August 2026** VRS publication date specified in this Additional Information.
- The Online Closed Qualifier awards **2** Offline LAN Main Event slots by default.
- If one or both Asia Direct VRS Invite LAN slots cannot be filled, the Online Closed Qualifier will award additional Offline LAN Main Event slots under Pathway A.
- The top finishing eligible rosters from the Online Closed Qualifier advance to the Offline LAN Main Event according to the number of LAN slots awarded through this pathway.
- Full Closed Qualifier rules are set out in **Closed Qualifier** below.

## Open Qualifier

- The Online Open Qualifier is restricted to eligible registered rosters from Asia.
- It awards Closed Qualifier slots only.

### Registration and schedule

- Online Open Qualifier registration opens on **August 7, 2026**.
- Online Open Qualifier registration closes on **August 30, 2026**.
- The Online Open Qualifier will begin on **August 31, 2026**.
- Online Open Qualifier registration is hosted via **FACEIT**.
- FACEIT championship URL: TBC.
- Entry into the Online Open Qualifier is free.
- Online Open Qualifier registration order and timestamps are publicly inspectable via **FACEIT**.

### Platform and format

- Online Open Qualifier matches are hosted on **FACEIT** using **FACEIT Anti-Cheat**, on servers in **Sydney, Australia**.
- Sydney is the online server location only and does not define Open Qualifier eligibility.
- The Online Open Qualifier will be double-elimination.
- Matches will be **Best-of-1** until qualification matches.
- Qualification matches will be **Best-of-3**.
- No roster-specific or discretionary selection criteria will be used.

### Eligibility

- The Online Open Qualifier is open only to registered rosters based in **Asia**, or whose participating players are physically able to compete from **Asia** for all Online Open Qualifier matches.
- For this event, the Tournament Organiser applies an Asia regional filter within the Asia VRS parent region framework.
- Merely connecting to Sydney servers from outside Asia does not satisfy the Online Open Qualifier eligibility requirements.
- A roster that does not satisfy the Online Open Qualifier regional eligibility requirements is not eligible to enter, be seeded into, advance from, or receive a Closed Qualifier slot through the Online Open Qualifier pathway.
- Roster eligibility for the Online Open Qualifier may be checked before seeding, during the qualifier, after the qualifier, and before Closed Qualifier confirmation.
- If an ineligible roster is mistakenly entered into the Online Open Qualifier bracket, that roster will be removed from Closed Qualifier slot contention once identified.
- If an ineligible roster finishes in a Closed Qualifier qualification position, the slot will pass to the next highest-placing eligible Asia roster from the Online Open Qualifier according to the published standings and tiebreaker procedure.
- This is an objective eligibility procedure. See **VRS Information** for invite method.

### Closed Qualifier qualification

- The top **8** eligible rosters from the Online Open Qualifier will advance to the Online Closed Qualifier.
- The Online Open Qualifier does not award Offline LAN Main Event slots directly.

## Vacancies & Replacements

### Invite acknowledgement and acceptance

- Direct VRS Invited Rosters have **48 hours** from invite issue to acknowledge the invite, and a further **48 hours** after acknowledgement to accept the invite (**4 days total** from invite issue).
- If a Direct VRS Invited Roster does not acknowledge within 48 hours, does not accept within the further 48 hours after acknowledgement, declines, cannot accept, fails eligibility checks, or fails roster validation, the vacant invite slot will be offered to the next highest-ranked eligible roster on the same announced VRS list, proceeding sequentially down the list.

### Slot supplementation

- If either or both of the **2 Asia** Direct VRS Invite LAN slots cannot be filled after proceeding sequentially down the Asia VRS list under the acknowledgement and acceptance process, each unfilled Asia LAN slot will be converted into an additional Offline LAN Main Event slot awarded through the Online Closed Qualifier.

### Qualifier withdrawals

- If a roster qualified through the Online Open Qualifier withdraws prior to the start of the Online Closed Qualifier, the vacant Closed Qualifier slot will be filled by the next highest-placing eligible roster from the Online Open Qualifier, as determined by final standings and predefined tiebreakers.
- If a roster qualified through the Online Closed Qualifier withdraws prior to the start of the Offline LAN Main Event, the vacant LAN slot will be filled by the next highest-placing eligible roster from the Online Closed Qualifier, as determined by final standings and predefined tiebreakers.

### Replacement metrics

- If no eligible roster is available from the relevant qualifier standings, the Tournament Organiser will select a replacement based on the highest-performing non-qualified roster from that qualifier using objective performance metrics, including:
  - final qualifier placement
  - match record
  - map differential
  - round differential
  - head-to-head result, where applicable
- All replacement decisions will be made using transparent and pre-defined criteria.
- No discretionary invites will be issued.

### Online Open Qualifier Ineligible Roster Procedure

- If a roster that does not satisfy the Online Open Qualifier regional eligibility requirements enters the Online Open Qualifier, advances through the bracket, or finishes in a Closed Qualifier qualification position, that roster will not receive an Online Closed Qualifier slot through the Online Open Qualifier pathway.
- The affected Closed Qualifier slot will be allocated to the next highest-placing eligible Asia roster from the Online Open Qualifier using the following objective order:
  1. final qualifier placement
  2. match record
  3. map differential
  4. round differential
  5. head-to-head result, where applicable
  6. any further tiebreaker defined in the official ruleset
- The same replacement ordering applies if a roster is removed by the Tournament Organiser before the start of the relevant stage.
- The full procedure is set out in **Vacancies & Replacements** in the official ruleset.
