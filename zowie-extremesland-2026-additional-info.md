## Tournament Format

### Summary

- **Tournament classification:** Ranked Tournament
- **Tournament Tier:** Tier 2
- **Main Event teams:** 12
- **Main Event composition:** 8 Regional Qualifier winners + 4 Direct VRS Invites
- **Ranked stages:** Open Qualifiers, Closed Qualifiers, and Offline LAN Main Event
- **Online stages:** Regional Qualifier stages marked Online in **Tournament Dates**
- **LAN stages:** Regional Qualifier stages marked LAN in **Tournament Dates**, plus the Offline LAN Main Event

### Stage 1 - Offline LAN Main Event Group Stage

- **Stage type:** LAN
- **Teams:** 8
- **Composition:** 1st place from each of the 8 Regional Qualifiers
- **Format:** GSL. 2 groups of 4. Grand Finals inside each group are not played.
- **Match format:** Opening matches and winners matches Best-of-1; elimination and decider matches Best-of-3
- **Group draw:** See **Seeding and Slot Allocation**
- **Advancement:** Top 2 teams from each group advance to Stage 2. The remaining 4 teams are eliminated from the Main Event.

Direct VRS Invites do not enter Stage 1.

### Stage 2 - Offline LAN Main Event Group Stage

- **Stage type:** LAN
- **Teams:** 8
- **Composition:** Top 2 from each Stage 1 group, plus 4 Direct VRS Invites
- **Format:** GSL. 2 groups of 4. Grand Finals inside each group are not played.
- **Match format:** Opening matches and winners matches Best-of-1; elimination and decider matches Best-of-3
- **Group draw:** See **Seeding and Slot Allocation**
- **Advancement:** Top 2 teams from each group advance to Stage 3

Regional Qualifier winners play Stage 1. Direct VRS Invites join at Stage 2.

### Stage 3 - Offline LAN Main Event Finals

- **Stage type:** LAN
- **Teams:** 4
- **Format:** Single-elimination
- **Match format:** Best-of-3
- **Pairings:**
  - 1st Group A vs 2nd Group B
  - 1st Group B vs 2nd Group A

### Platform and venue

- **Offline venue:** TBC
- **Visa region:** China

### Map pool

Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage, Nuke.

The pool may change with notice from the Tournament Organiser if the active duty map pool is updated.

### Pathway A - Regional Qualifiers

Each Regional Qualifier awards **1** Offline LAN Main Event Stage 1 slot.

A roster may compete in only one region for the entirety of The Event.

Regional assignment uses **citizenship**. If a roster has players from more than one region, the citizenship that holds a **plurality** is the roster's regional assignment. If two regions are tied, the roster may select only one region. That selection applies for the entirety of The Event.

Eligible countries are listed under **Open Qualifier**.

Stage rules for each region are set out in **Open Qualifier**.

### Pathway B - Direct VRS Invites

- **Direct VRS Invites:** 4
- **Enter:** Offline LAN Main Event Stage 2
- **VRS list:** Asia
- **Invitation Date:** Monday, November 2, 2026
- **VRS publication date used for invites:** November 2, 2026
- **VRS rank from which invitations begin:** 1st
- **Filter:** Rosters ranked 1st to 12th on Global VRS at the Invitation Date are not eligible
- **VRS subdivision used:** None. Invites use the Asia parent list. They are not split between Oceania, West Asia, and East Asia.

Invitations proceed sequentially down the Asia VRS list until the 4 Direct VRS Invite slots are filled.

A Direct VRS Invited Roster may accept only if:

- at least 3 of the 5 players listed on the Invited Roster will play each match
- the Participating Roster satisfies the invite criteria
- the roster has not previously declined an invitation to this tournament

This is a Direct VRS Invite process. It is separate from Regional Qualifier qualification.

## Seeding and Slot Allocation

### Regional Qualifiers

- Regional Qualifier seeding uses the most recent VRS Rankings available prior to commencement of that region's matches.
- Rosters without a Global VRS rank cannot be seeded above VRS-ranked rosters.

Where a region uses the published GSL group draw, groups are:

- **Group A:** Seeds 1, 4, 5, 8. Opening matches 1 vs 8 and 4 vs 5
- **Group B:** Seeds 2, 3, 6, 7. Opening matches 2 vs 7 and 3 vs 6

That draw is used for Mongolia Closed Qualifier, Oceania Closed Qualifier, Main Event Stage 1, and Main Event Stage 2.

Where a 4-team finals bracket is cross-seeded, pairings are:

- 1st Group A vs 2nd Group B
- 1st Group B vs 2nd Group A

That pairing is used for Mongolia Closed Qualifier Finals, Oceania Closed Qualifier Finals, and Main Event Stage 3.

### Main Event

- Main Event seeding uses the **December 7, 2026** VRS Rankings.
- Stage 1 seeds the 8 Regional Qualifier winners into two GSL groups using that snapshot.
- Stage 2 seeds the 4 Stage 1 advancers and the 4 Direct VRS Invites into two GSL groups using that snapshot.
- Rosters without a Global VRS rank cannot be seeded above VRS-ranked rosters.
- A Directly Invited VRS Roster cannot start at a later stage than another Directly Invited VRS Roster with a higher VRS rank. All 4 Direct VRS Invites enter Stage 2.

## Vacancies & Replacements

### Regional Qualifier Main Event slots

If a qualified Regional Qualifier roster withdraws prior to the replacement deadline, the slot will be offered to the next highest-placed team from that same Regional Qualifier.

If a clear next highest-placed team cannot be determined, tiebreakers are:

1. Head-to-head win/loss, where possible
2. Match win/loss difference
3. Map win/loss difference
4. Round win/loss difference
5. Initial seed
6. Coinflip

### Direct VRS Invite slots

If a Direct VRS Invited Roster cannot accept or declines, invitations proceed sequentially down the announced Asia VRS list until the announced Direct VRS Invite slots are filled.

If fewer eligible VRS rosters exist than announced Direct VRS Invite slots, remaining slots may be filled through Open Qualifiers. Any such supplement will be published before it is used.

No discretionary invites will be issued.

## Roster Continuity

### Direct VRS Invited Rosters

- A forfeit will be declared in any match where a roster does not field at least three of the Invited Roster Athletes for the entirety of the match.
- Teams must maintain a minimum of 3 of the 5 players listed on the invited roster in each match.

### Open Qualifier and Closed Qualifier rosters

- Teams which qualify for a Closed Qualifier must maintain the same roster as in their successful Open Qualifier.
- Teams are permitted to have up to two substitutes on their roster during Open Qualifiers and Closed Qualifiers.

### Main Event rosters

- Teams which qualify for the Main Event may submit roster changes for approval by the Tournament Organiser. The roster must remain eligible under the published eligibility rules.

### Coaches

- **Online matches:** Only the 5 players may be in the game server and voice communications for the duration of the match.
- **Offline (LAN) matches:** The coach may be present behind the team and in voice communications, and may speak only during tactical pauses, halftimes, and outside of matches.
