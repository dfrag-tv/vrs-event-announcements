## Open Qualifier

The Online Open Qualifier is an official stage of this tournament. It is subject to the official ruleset and this Additional Information. Those documents are governed by the applicable Valve Tournament Operation Requirements.

The Online Open Qualifier is not a ranked VRS stage. Global VRS is used only as the published seeding method.

The Online Open Qualifier is restricted to eligible Oceania rosters whose participating players are residents of Australia or New Zealand.

### Registration and schedule

- Online Open Qualifier registration opens on **September 11, 2026, 00:00 AEST (UTC+10)**.
- Online Open Qualifier registration closes on **September 18, 2026, 23:55 AEST (UTC+10)**.
- Online Open Qualifier play starts on **September 19, 2026, 10:00 AEST (UTC+10)** and continues on **September 20, 2026**.
- Online Open Qualifier registration is hosted via **FACEIT** using the Open Registration URL in Qualification Pathways.
- Entry is free.

### Platform and format

- Matches are hosted on **FACEIT** using **FACEIT Anti-Cheat**, on servers in **Sydney, Australia**.
- The Open Qualifier is **single-elimination**.
- The final Open Qualifier bracket size will be published after registration closes and before play begins.
- Matches will be **Best-of-1** until qualification matches.
- Qualification matches will be **Best-of-3**.
- Open Qualifier seeding uses Global VRS-ranked rosters highest to lowest, then unranked rosters by FACEIT Elo, then FACEIT's deterministic process or a recorded random draw where required.
- A roster without a Global VRS rank cannot be seeded above a VRS-ranked roster.
- No roster-specific or discretionary selection criteria will be used.

### Eligibility

- The Online Open Qualifier is restricted to eligible Oceania rosters whose participating players are residents of **Australia** or **New Zealand**.
- Connecting to Sydney servers from another country does not satisfy residency eligibility.
- Roster eligibility may be checked before seeding, during the qualifier, after the qualifier, and before Stage 2 confirmation.
- If an ineligible roster finishes in a Stage 2 qualification position, the slot will pass to the next highest-placing eligible roster from the Online Open Qualifier according to the published standings and tiebreaker procedure.

### Stage 2 qualification

- The top 8 eligible rosters from the Online Open Qualifier will advance to the Stage 2 Elimination Stage.

## Seeding and Slot Allocation

- Stage 2 slots are filled only by the top 8 eligible Online Open Qualifier rosters.
- On **September 25, 2026**, those 8 rosters will be seeded using the September 2026 official VRS publication named above.
- Ranked rosters will be ordered using **Global VRS ranking** from that publication.
- Rosters without a Global VRS rank cannot be seeded above VRS-ranked rosters.
- A lower-ranked Global VRS roster cannot be seeded above a higher-ranked Global VRS roster.
- VRS-ranked rosters are ordered from highest to lowest Global VRS ranking, then unranked rosters.
- Intended Stage 2 group distribution:
  - **Group A:** Seeds 1, 4, 5, 8
  - **Group B:** Seeds 2, 3, 6, 7
- If no participating Stage 2 roster has a Global VRS ranking, tournament administration will conduct and record a random draw.
- Stage 3 pairings are Group A #1 vs Group B #2 and Group B #1 vs Group A #2.

## Vacancies & Replacements

Where a qualified team withdraws, is declared ineligible or is disqualified before entering the Elimination Stage, the vacant position will be offered using the following objective order:

1. Highest finishing non-qualified Open Qualifier team.
2. Highest Open Qualifier stage reached.
3. Match differential.
4. Map differential.
5. Round differential.
6. Head-to-head result, where applicable.
7. Initial published Open Qualifier seed.
8. Recorded random draw.

No discretionary invites will be issued.

Where a vacancy occurs after Elimination Stage competition begins, the published bracket and forfeiture rules apply unless an objective replacement procedure was published before tournament play began.

- Roster lock is **September 24, 2026**. After roster lock, roster changes are not allowed.
- A registered substitute cannot be changed after roster lock. Only a substitute already named on the locked roster may be selected into a match lineup.
- A coach may occupy the coach slot only in extraordinary circumstances, as defined in the official ruleset. A coach cannot be added, removed or replaced after roster lock.
